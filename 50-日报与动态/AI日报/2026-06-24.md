### 1. Anthropic隐藏安全措施政策大逆转

**事件概述**:
Anthropic在遭遇强烈反弹后,撤回了Claude Fable 5的隐藏安全干预政策。

**原政策(已废除)**:
> "These safeguards will not be visible to the user. Fable 5 will not fall back to a different model. Instead, the safeguards will limit effectiveness through methods such as prompt modification, steering vectors, or parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT)."

当用户询问ML加速器设计、预训练管线、分布式训练基础设施时,模型会静默降低回答质量,而不告知用户。

**新政策(本周开始)**:
> "We're rolling out changes to make Fable 5's safeguards for frontier LLM development visible. Starting this week, flagged requests will visibly fall back to Opus 4.8... We went with invisible safeguards for this reason—and that was the wrong tradeoff."

**影响范围**: ~0.03%流量,不到0.1%的组织受影响

**分析**:
这是frontier模型首次尝试"隐形退化"机制,与传统的明确拒绝不同。争议点在于:
- 打破了用户对模型输出一致性的信任
- 开了静默修改响应的先例
- 影响技术研发透明度

**来源**: Simon Willison's Weblog - "Anthropic Walks Back Hidden AI Safeguards"

### 3. 2026 Frontier模型Benchmark对比(持续热议)

#### 核心数据

| Benchmark | Fable 5 | Opus 4.8 | GPT-5.5 | Gemini 3.1 Pro | Grok 4 |
|-----------|---------|----------|---------|----------------|--------|
| **SWE-Bench Pro** | 80.3% | - | 58.6% | 54.2% | ~75% |
| **FrontierCode Diamond** | 29.3% | 13.4% | 5.7% | - | - |

**价格**:
- Fable 5: $10/M input tokens, $50/M output tokens
- Opus 4.8: Fable 5的一半价格

**实战反馈**(Simon Willison测试):
- "a _beast_" - 5.5小时深度测试
- 慢且贵,但处理复杂任务能力强
- 挑战在于找到它"做不到"的任务
- 单次生成近2400 tokens仅用4.4秒

**工程选型建议**:
- **选Fable 5**: 需要顶级coding准确度(80.3% SWE-Bench Pro),预算充足,可接受5%的安全干预触发率
- **选Opus 4.8**: 需要强coding+agentic性能,成本敏感,不需要Fable 5的极端准确度
- **选GPT-5.5**: 执行一致性高,但规划能力略弱于Claude系

**新兴实践**(企业环境):
```
Planning: Opus 4.8 / Fable 5 (wider aperture, downstream effects awareness)
Execution: GPT-5.5 (consistent, direct)
```
